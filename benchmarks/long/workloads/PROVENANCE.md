## Fixtures and independent checks

- The movie oracle uses manually annotated source cells. It counts joined membership pairs directly, with individually verified examples of malformed cells, literal versus numeric IDs, repeated credits and tied rankings.
- The KNN oracle ranks neighbors once per row and metric. Its audit fixture has three distinct center labels and fifteen non-center points labeled `z`. Each held-out training fold leaves two conflicting zero-distance labels at the center. Correct counts and holdout predictions for this fixture were also worked out by hand.
- The pack oracle hashes known canonical CSV header and record byte segments. For the sorted golden it stable-sorts the entire repeated unsorted input. An automated test cross-checks the manifest against the materialized files generated by the original fixture utility.
- A valid answer is a single JSON value. The checker ignores object key order and JSON whitespace. It checks exact scalar types, including integer counts, and rejects duplicate keys, nonfinite constants, and trailing non-whitespace text. Four variants are available. `base`, `edges` and `profile` are used for development and timing. `audit` is reserved for independent verification after generation and repair.
